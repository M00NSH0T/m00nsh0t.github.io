How to read this

PPO has a policy network that chooses allocations and a value network that estimates future reward. Value loss measures its prediction error; it is not Q-learning error. Lower loss alone does not imply better investing. Entropy describes the raw Gaussian action distribution; requested allocation dispersion measures variation after clipping and normalization. Executed holdings can differ because of costs, available cash and wash-sale guards.

KL divergence measures how much the policy changes per update. Clip fraction measures how often PPO limits an update. Explained variance measures value prediction quality: near 1 is stronger, around 0 is weak, and negative is worse than a constant predictor. Episode reward is cumulative log growth, averaged over the most recent completed training episodes. All training charts use cumulative environment steps on the horizontal axis.
