Checking corrected corporate-action results…

Daily retraining has not shown a consistent advantage across training seeds, including comparisons with similar training budgets. Correlated-peer rules have not beaten their simpler controls reliably. Use these results to choose the next experiment, rather than a live strategy.

News and a pre-test-period sentiment model have now been tested. Daily execution improves the combined-stock IRA candidate, but higher costs reduce it below VOO. One of three shuffled-sentiment controls earns more with a deeper drawdown, so the signal itself remains unproven. The IRA view retains the candidate alongside cost, execution and passive controls. The default taxable view compares a lower-trade volatility rule with VOO and SPY; borrowing did not improve every rule and none of these highlighted variants beat VOO on return.
