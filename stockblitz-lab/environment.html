Earlier feature audit · research rationale

Some normalization exists; generalization is not demonstrated. Price movements use log ratios: a 1% gain has the same return feature at $10 and $100. The price-rescaling probe passed for return blocks. Raw price, volume and elapsed ages still have inconsistent scales, and the encoder depends on fixed stock and lot positions.

What should change before larger training runs?
  • Use trailing volatility and relative volume, with training-only normalization, availability flags and explicit extreme-move information.
  • Share an encoder across stocks and treat tax lots as a set, so changing their listing order does not create a different economic situation.
  • Combine minute, five-minute and daily/weekly paths. The current short LSTM windows and long-return snapshots do not retain a full market movie.
  • Represent news with both a text embedding and explicit facts: event type, actor, affected companies, numeric surprise, timing, uncertainty and source evidence. A competitor price increase and an earnings beat should remain distinguishable.
  • Retain multiple events and known competitor/supplier relationships. Test issuer/sector holdouts, chronological folds and feature-removal controls.

These are proposed changes. Archived policies retain their original representation; artificial news is not real article understanding. Historic articles processed by a modern model can contain knowledge of later outcomes through model training.

What the agent can do

Hold submits no orders. Otherwise choose one account, one security, and buy/reduce by 1%, 5% or 20% of account equity, or close the position. Changes in prices do not trigger automatic rebalancing. Orders can be rejected or partially filled.

Five-minute decisions use completed observations; simulated fills use a raw minute open one minute into the following bar. This is a price proxy, not a measured midpoint. Commission, dated sell fees, settlement, supported taxes and configured financing affect wealth. There is no hold bonus or trading penalty.

Why the first fresh models failed

On twelve additional artificial scenarios, three of four deterministic policies repeatedly traded and exhausted settled buying power before the jump in all nine opportunity cases. The fourth held cash throughout. Exploratory policies averaged 20–24 orders per episode; the observable-cue reference averaged 1.3. This points to an exploration and delayed-reward problem, not evidence that taxes or settlement should be removed.

The completed joint-policy test began with 95% Hold probability instead of 50%. Both seeds then chose cash in every deterministic evaluation and missed all opportunities. Sampled trading fell to 0.4–3 orders per episode, without improving returns. All feasible orders remain available and probabilities can change during learning. Rewards, execution costs, taxes, observations and the 65,536-transition budget remain unchanged. It is a diagnostic, not an enforced holding rule. The next investigation is exploration that persists long enough to experience a payoff while remaining responsive to new signals. The cash lockout above applies to this conservative cash-account fixture; IRA reinvestment and taxable margin modes differ.

What the agent sees

Minute and five-minute sequences feed separate LSTMs. Longer-horizon return context, company correlations, account cash and positions, individual tax lots, pending wash losses and dated news features remain explicit. The model has a finite visible window; it does not remember an entire episode indefinitely.

The current real-data smoke test has no news feed supplied. Missing minute/news inputs are marked unavailable. This prototype does not yet include fundamentals, cross-company attention or verified extended-hours execution.

Tax coverage and boundaries

2026 married-filing-jointly standard-deduction scenario: federal ordinary/preferential rates, NIIT, New York State recapture and NYC tax. Known-account identical-share wash chains include partial replacements and permanent IRA disallowance. Losses stay provisional until their forward window resolves.

Reported wealth marks open positions at current prices and deducts accrued tax on realized activity. It does not deduct hypothetical tax on selling remaining positions or withdrawing IRA funds. Those are separate, unfinished valuation scenarios.

The model rejects income outside its supported range. It is not a tax-return engine. Itemization, AMT preferences, foreign income, general multi-year rollovers, substantially-identical cross-security matching and full dividend/split interactions remain incomplete. Household inputs stay in ignored local files.
